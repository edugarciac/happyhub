## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Home gallery shows the real venue
The homepage gallery SHALL show real photos of the HappyHub venue, and portrait photos SHALL be shown uncropped.

#### Scenario: Visitor browses the gallery
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the homepage gallery
- **THEN** they SHALL see real photos of the hall, play area, chill zone and restrooms

#### Scenario: Portrait photo in the carousel
- **WHEN** a portrait photo is the current slide
- **THEN** it SHALL be displayed whole over a blurred background, not cropped to 16:9
