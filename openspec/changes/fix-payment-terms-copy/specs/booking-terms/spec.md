## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Remaining balance payment timing copy
The system SHALL consistently state, on every screen and message where the remaining balance timing is mentioned, that it is due before the event begins — not on the day of the event in general.

#### Scenario: Customer reviews terms and conditions in Step 3
- **WHEN** a customer views the terms and conditions section during booking
- **THEN** the text SHALL read "El resto del pago se realizará antes de comenzar el evento."

#### Scenario: Customer reviews the generated contract PDF
- **WHEN** a customer or admin views the contract PDF generated by `generateContractPDF`
- **THEN** the terms and conditions section SHALL read "El resto del importe debe abonarse antes de comenzar el evento."

#### Scenario: Customer views their reservation status page
- **WHEN** a customer views `/mi-reserva/[id]` with a pending remaining balance
- **THEN** the text SHALL read "A pagar antes de comenzar el evento"

#### Scenario: Customer receives the WhatsApp confirmation message
- **WHEN** a reservation confirmation is sent via WhatsApp
- **THEN** the remaining-balance line SHALL read "a pagar antes de comenzar el evento"

#### Scenario: Customer reads the public pricing/terms/FAQ pages
- **WHEN** a visitor reads `/disponibilidad`, `/terminos`, `/como-funciona`, `PricingTable`, `PriceSummary`, or `Step4Confirmation`
- **THEN** any mention of the remaining balance timing SHALL read "antes de comenzar el evento" instead of "el día del evento"
