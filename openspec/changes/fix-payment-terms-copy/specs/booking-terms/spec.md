## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Remaining balance payment timing copy
The system SHALL display terms and conditions copy in the booking flow stating that the remaining balance is due before the event begins, not on the day of the event in general.

#### Scenario: Customer reviews terms and conditions in Step 3
- **WHEN** a customer views the terms and conditions section during booking
- **THEN** the text SHALL read "El resto del pago se realizará antes de comenzar el evento."
