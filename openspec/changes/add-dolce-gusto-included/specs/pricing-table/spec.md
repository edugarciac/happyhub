## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Included items in the price
The public pricing table SHALL list what the base price includes: exclusive use of the space, basic furniture (tables and chairs), sound system, microwave and fridge, and a Dolce Gusto coffee machine.

#### Scenario: Visitor checks what is included
- **WHEN** a visitor views "¿Qué incluye el precio?" in the "Tarifas" section
- **THEN** the list SHALL include "Cafetera Dolce Gusto"
