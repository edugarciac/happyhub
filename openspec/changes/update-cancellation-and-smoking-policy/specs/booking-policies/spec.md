## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Cancellation policy wording
Every surface that states the cancellation policy (home pricing, availability, FAQ, contact, terms, contract, PDF, customer reservation page) SHALL state the same three tiers: full deposit refund at 15 or more days before the event; no refund but date change allowed (subject to availability) between 14 and 3 days; no refund and no date change under 3 days.

#### Scenario: Visitor compares pages
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the cancellation policy on any page or document
- **THEN** the tiers SHALL match and no page SHALL mention a 30-day date-change window

### Requirement: Smoking and vaping rule
Terms, FAQ, contract and PDF SHALL state that smoking cigarettes and using vapers or e-cigarettes inside the venue is prohibited.
