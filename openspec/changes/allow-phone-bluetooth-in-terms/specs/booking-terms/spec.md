## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Own audio equipment versus phone by Bluetooth
The terms and the contract SHALL forbid bringing or connecting the customer's own audio equipment, and SHALL explicitly allow playing music from a phone by Bluetooth through the room's sound system.

#### Scenario: Customer reads the terms before booking
- **WHEN** a customer reads the terms in the booking form
- **THEN** they SHALL see that speakers or mixers of their own are not allowed
- **AND** that playing music from their phone by Bluetooth is allowed

#### Scenario: Acceptance evidence
- **WHEN** a customer accepts the updated terms
- **THEN** the acceptance SHALL be stored with terms version "25 de octubre de 2026"
