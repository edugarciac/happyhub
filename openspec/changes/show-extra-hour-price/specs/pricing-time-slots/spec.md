## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Extra hour price is shown clearly
The system SHALL show that each additional hour extending the afternoon slot (up to 23:00h) costs 60 €.

#### Scenario: Visitor views the pricing table
- **WHEN** a visitor views the pricing table
- **THEN** its footer SHALL state that each additional hour costs 60 €

#### Scenario: Visitor reads the FAQ
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the FAQ answers about time slots and extending the schedule
- **THEN** they SHALL state that each additional hour costs 60 €
