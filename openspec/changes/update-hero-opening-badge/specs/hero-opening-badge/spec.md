## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Hero opening date badge
The homepage Hero SHALL display a prominent badge announcing the confirmed opening date of the venue.

#### Scenario: Visitor sees correct opening date
- **WHEN** a visitor loads the homepage
- **THEN** the badge SHALL display the text "Abrimos a partir de Septiembre de 2026"

#### Scenario: Badge stands out visually
- **WHEN** the badge is rendered over the Hero video background
- **THEN** it SHALL use a solid, high-contrast color (not a translucent background) so it is immediately noticeable
